Belfast PAT testing questions

Before you book

Do you provide PAT testing in Belfast?

Yes. We cover businesses, landlords and organisations throughout Belfast from our Ballygawley base.

How much does PAT testing cost in Belfast?

Normal pricing is £85 for the first 20 items and £2 per additional item, plus a £30 Belfast travel charge. Belfast bookings have a £150 minimum total.

Can you test while a Belfast office or shop is operating?

Often, yes. The visit can be planned around quieter periods and access, with equipment disconnected briefly where testing requires it.

Can several Belfast sites be tested together?

Yes. Multi-site or route bookings can be planned together. Send the addresses and approximate item counts so the work can be organised efficiently.

Can testing be arranged outside normal opening hours?

Yes. Evening and weekend appointments can be arranged for an additional £30, subject to availability.

What paperwork is supplied?

Completed, paid work includes pass/fail labels, an appliance register and a certificate/report for your records.

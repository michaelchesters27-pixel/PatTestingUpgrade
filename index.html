All FAQs

Will you travel for a small booking?

Yes. Small bookings are accepted throughout Northern Ireland, provided the applicable travel charge is agreed before the visit.

What is included?

Visual inspection, electrical testing where appropriate, pass/fail labels, an appliance register and certificate/report for completed, paid work.

Can testing be completed outside opening hours?

Yes. Evening and weekend appointments can be arranged for an additional £30.

What if an appliance fails?

The item is clearly identified and recorded so it can be removed from use, repaired or replaced as appropriate.

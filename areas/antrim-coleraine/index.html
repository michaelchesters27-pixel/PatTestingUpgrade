Before booking in Antrim & Coleraine

How is travel to Antrim, Ballymena or Coleraine calculated?

The travel charge is confirmed before booking after the exact address and route are known. The online estimate clearly shows travel as “to confirm”.

Can several north-coast premises be booked together?

Yes. Multiple sites or neighbouring businesses can be planned as a route day when addresses and item estimates are supplied.

Do you cover hospitality and accommodation businesses?

Yes. Portable electrical equipment in cafés, hospitality venues and managed accommodation can be inspected, tested and recorded.

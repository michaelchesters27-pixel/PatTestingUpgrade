What can be tested

Portable electrical equipment commonly found on site

The exact testing required depends on the equipment, its construction, condition and environment. A practical visit combines visual inspection with electrical testing where appropriate.

  • Kitchen and refreshment equipment
  • Sound, audiovisual and stage equipment
  • Office computers and printers
  • Portable heaters and fans
  • Extension leads and cable reels
  • Cleaning and maintenance appliances

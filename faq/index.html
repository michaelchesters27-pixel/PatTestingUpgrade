PAT testing questions answered

Straight answers about pricing, coverage, the testing visit and the records supplied.

Request a quoteWhatsApp

How much does PAT testing cost?

The first 20 items cost £85. Each additional item costs £2. Travel charges apply according to location and are shown or confirmed before booking.

Will you travel anywhere in Northern Ireland for a small job?

Yes. Small bookings are accepted across Northern Ireland, provided the applicable travel charge is agreed before the visit.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes. Out-of-hours appointments can be arranged for an additional £30, subject to availability.

What is included in the visit?

The service includes visual inspection, electrical testing where appropriate, pass/fail labels, an appliance register and a certificate/report for completed, paid work.

What happens when an appliance fails?

The item is clearly identified and recorded so it can be removed from use, repaired or replaced as appropriate.

Do all electrical appliances require the same test?

No. The appropriate inspection and test depend on the appliance construction, condition and environment. Equipment is assessed individually.

Can you test while my business is open?

Often, yes. The visit can be planned around access and quieter periods. Salons, cafés and other busy premises may prefer an out-of-hours appointment.

Do you test extension leads and cable reels?

Yes. Extension leads, multiway adaptors and cable reels are common items on PAT testing visits.

Do you cover landlords and rental properties?

Yes. Portable appliances supplied in furnished rental properties and managed accommodation can be inspected, tested and recorded.

How should I prepare for the appointment?

Make equipment accessible, identify items that belong to the premises and let staff know that appliances may need to be unplugged briefly during testing.

Is your test equipment calibrated?

The business follows an annual equipment calibration programme to support reliable testing records.

Are you insured and qualified?

Yes. PAT Testing Tyrone provides a qualified service with public liability insurance and 25 years of electrical experience.

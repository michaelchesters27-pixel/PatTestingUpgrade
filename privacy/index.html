Privacy policy

How information submitted through this website is used and protected.

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Information collected

When you request a quote, the website may collect your name, business or organisation name, email address, phone number, location, approximate item count and any notes you submit.

How it is used

The information is used to respond to your enquiry, calculate or confirm a price, arrange a booking and provide the requested service. It is not sold to third parties.

Website forms

Quote forms are processed through the website hosting platform. Avoid including confidential or sensitive information in the notes field.

Retention

Enquiry and customer records may be retained where reasonably necessary for quotations, bookings, business administration, testing records and legal or accounting obligations.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information where applicable. Contact michael@pattestingtyrone.co.uk.

External links

The website links to Google, WhatsApp and other third-party services. Their own privacy policies apply when you use those services.

Contact

PAT Testing Tyrone is based in Ballygawley, County Tyrone. Email michael@pattestingtyrone.co.uk or call 07379 327277.

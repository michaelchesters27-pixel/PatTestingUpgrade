Newry PAT testing questions

Before you book

Do you provide PAT testing in Newry?

Yes. We cover businesses, landlords and organisations throughout Newry from our Ballygawley base.

How much does PAT testing cost in Newry?

Normal pricing is £85 for the first 20 items and £2 per additional item, plus a £30 travel charge. Newry bookings have a £150 minimum total.

Can several Newry sites be tested together?

Yes. Multi-site or route bookings can be planned together when addresses and approximate item counts are provided.

Can testing be arranged outside opening hours?

Yes. Evening and weekend appointments can be arranged for an additional £30, subject to availability.
